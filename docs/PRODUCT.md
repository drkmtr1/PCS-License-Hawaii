# Product

## Mission

Help military-connected professionals relocating to Hawaiʻi identify official licensing information and the next official action in under five minutes, without representing that the application determines legal eligibility or authorization to practice.

## Primary users

- Military spouses relocating to Hawaiʻi because of military orders.
- Servicemembers relocating to Hawaiʻi who hold civilian professional credentials.

## Problem

Official information is distributed across DCCA/PVL board and program pages, PDFs, statutes and rules, MyPVL, federal SCRA guidance, and compact authorities. The route and application channel vary by profession. Current federal and state-facing materials also differ in how they describe portability requirements.

## V1 intervention

Choose a supported profession, answer only material questions, and receive a deterministic navigation result containing potential pathways, why they may be relevant, likely documents, official destinations, cited sources, freshness/conflict warnings, unresolved questions, and a responsible authority contact.

## Frozen V1 profession scope

1. RN/LPN — multiple distinct routes: endorsement, employment-dependent temporary permit, and temporary military-spouse licensing.
2. Mental health counselor — general licensing and temporary military-spouse licensing, with a new provisional pathway that makes freshness especially important.
3. Electrician — board approval, experience/coursework, and examination workflow; no profession-specific temporary military-spouse form was located in Stage 0.
4. Real estate salesperson/broker — no interstate reciprocity; qualified out-of-state licensees may pursue prelicense-education, uniform-examination, and (for brokers) experience equivalencies before the Hawaiʻi-specific examination route. No profession-specific temporary military-spouse form was located in Stage 0.

The last two professions are included to prevent the product from implying that every profession has the same state pathway.

## Out of scope

Accounts, saved cases, document upload, application submission, eligibility determinations, all-profession coverage, automatic source ingestion, administrative CMS, nationwide comparison, and runtime AI.

## Success hypothesis

On approved representative scenarios, 100% of safety-critical pathway/source assertions are correct, 100% of cases outside approved evidence abstain, and no result claims the application authorized practice. The usability hypothesis is at least 95% task completion with the next official source reached in under five minutes; results must also be reported by profession, route, and military relationship.
