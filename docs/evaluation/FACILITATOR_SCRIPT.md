## Before the session

- Confirm informed consent and separate recording consent.
- Use the approved clean browser/device profile and assigned accessible/translated materials.
- Verify assistive technology and recording scope with the participant.
- Remind the participant to use only fictional facts and not disclose a real license, order, employer, case history, or identifier.

## Opening

“This is a navigation study using fictional facts. It is not legal or licensing advice and cannot tell anyone whether they may practice. Please do not use your own information. You may stop participating at any time without penalty. If you want already-collected study data removed or a recording redacted, that request is available only through the deadline and subject to the limits stated in the consent form. We will stop before any login, application, download containing personal data, email, call, purchase, or submission.”

## Permitted facilitator responses

- Repeat the fictional scenario or task verbatim.
- Clarify controls or accessibility needs without explaining licensing content.
- Pause/stop for participant safety, consent, technical failure, or prohibited destination.

## Prohibited assistance

- Do not name a pathway, interpret a source, suggest a search term/link, resolve a conflict, or validate an answer.
- Do not allow search engines, AI summaries, private advice sites, saved accounts/history, or the product domain in BL-001.
- Do not allow contact or submission to any authority/vendor.

## Timing and observation

- Start at task reveal.
- Mark the first correct next official source and five-minute status.
- Stop at 10 minutes and record full-completion status.
- Record wrong turns, assistance requests, inaccessible content, terminology, redirects, and stop-at-destination events without adding interpretation.

## Close

Read the approved debrief. Repeat that stopping participation is available at any time without penalty, and explain the consent form's deadline and limits for removal of already-collected data or recording redaction. Confirm any request without promising action beyond the approved process. Do not discuss whether the fictional or real person is eligible to practice.
