## Consent fields

- Study purpose and fictional navigation tasks.
- Procedures, duration, condition assignment, and browser/screen activity.
- Voluntary participation, skip/stop/withdraw process, and withdrawal deadline/effect.
- Foreseeable risks: confusing licensing information, accidental personal disclosure, recording/identifiability, accessibility burden, and external-site content.
- Compensation and whether withdrawal affects it.
- Data collected, purpose, restricted pseudonymized status, recording scope, access, encryption/transfer, retention/deletion, backup handling, incident contact, and aggregate reporting.
- Separate choices for audio, screen, and video/face recording; default to minimum/no-face capture.
- Accessibility/language accommodations and neutral interpreter role.
- Research contact and concerns/incident contact.
- Explicit agreement not to use real personal facts or take application/contact/purchase actions.
- Consent confirmation, date, exact approved document version and commit SHA, and participant ID. Store identity/signature separately from session data.

## Required no-reliance statement

“The scenario and findings are fictional research exercises. They are not legal or licensing advice, do not establish eligibility or authority to practice, and must not be used to make a real application or work decision. Only the responsible authority can make that determination.”

## Debrief

- Repeat the no-reliance statement.
- Explain whether the participant used official resources or a prototype without revealing other participants' conditions.
- Repeat that participation may stop at any time without penalty; offer the approved deadline and limits for withdrawal/redaction of already-collected data.
- Provide only the approved general official-resource starting bundle for future independent use.
- Do not reveal the oracle answer or discuss the participant's real circumstances.
